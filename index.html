RULES

Goal

Survive as long as you can and score big. One touch from an enemy or from your own echo ends the run.

Your echo

  • An echo ghost retraces your exact path, 2.4 seconds behind you.
  • A bright line ahead of it shows the route it will take next.
  • It is deadly to you, but it destroys every wisp and brute it touches.
  • New echoes start dormant, with a ring filling at the start point, before they awaken.

Enemies

  • Wisps (pink) home in on you. They appear as faint rings for about a second before they can hurt you or be killed.
  • Brutes (orange hexagons, after about 22 seconds) are slower and take two echo hits.
  • The longer you survive, the faster and more frequent they get.

Chains and score

  • Every wisp your echo sweeps scores points. Each explosion also destroys wisps nearby, so chain reactions give bonus points.
  • Quick kills raise your multiplier from ×1 up to ×12. It resets after 2.2 seconds with no kill.
  • You also earn a little for every second you stay alive.

Orbs

  • Gold orbs give points and keep your multiplier alive.
  • 5 orbs awaken a second echo (violet, 4.8 s behind you). 12 orbs awaken a third (gold, 7.2 s behind). More echoes mean more killing power but more danger.

Phase

  • Press Space, click, tap PHASE, or tap with a second finger to become intangible for 0.75 seconds and pass through enemies and echoes. The cooldown is 4 seconds, shown as a ring around you.

Controls

  • Move: mouse, WASD / arrow keys, or on a phone touch and drag anywhere to summon a joystick
  • Pause: P or Esc · Rules: H or ? · Restart after death: Enter

Tips

  • Run in wide loops. Tight circles run you into your own echo.
  • Gather wisps behind you, then curve away so your echo sweeps through them.
  • Save Phase for when an echo and a swarm close in together.
